Warm Outreach — How It Works

Your warm outreach system runs automatically in the background. Three times a week, it checks your contact list and queues personalized emails — birthday notes, regular check-ins, and seasonal holiday messages. Every email is written by AI in your voice, using the notes you have on file for each contact. Two days before any emails go out, you receive a notification at your Gmail with a count and a link to your review portal. You can log in, read each email, and flag anything that needs a correction. If you do nothing, emails go out automatically as scheduled.

Your review portal (live now): https://outreach.realestateandloans.com/warm-outreach

Testing Note

Note: During testing, holiday emails landed in Gmail's Promotions tab rather than the inbox. This is likely due to subject lines that read as group greetings (e.g. "Happy Thanksgiving, Mike and Karen"). Consider subject lines that feel more like a personal note — for example "Thinking of you this Thanksgiving" — to improve inbox placement. Addressing two people in the subject line is a known Gmail filter trigger.
